Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Shorts Uploader is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channels.

  • Data accessed: with the owner's permission, the tool uses Google OAuth to upload videos to the owner's YouTube channels and to read basic details of those channels (channel name and ID).
  • No other users: the tool is not offered to anyone else and collects no data about other people.
  • Storage: the owner's access credentials are stored privately by the owner (encrypted secrets) and are used only to perform uploads.
  • Sharing: no data is sold, shared or transferred to third parties.
  • Google API data: use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Revoking access: access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
